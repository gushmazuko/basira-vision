# Translator's Preface

This edition is a new translation, prepared from a scanned copy of the original book. The scanned pages were read with vision OCR, the recognized text was checked against the scan, and the book was then translated page by page, each page with the translation of the one before it in view, so that the text reads as one whole rather than as a series of separate pages.

The aim has been to convey the meaning of the original faithfully while keeping the language natural for today's reader. Proper names follow the conventions of the target language, and words that have no exact equivalent are explained briefly at their first occurrence. Where the scanned copy was damaged and a passage could not be read with confidence, this is marked in the text rather than guessed at.

The translation was prepared with the help of a language model and reviewed by hand before typesetting. Despite the care taken, errors may remain; where precision matters, readers are encouraged to consult the original.
